Worst drop is the largest fall in the account from a previous high. The Sharpe ratio is return per unit of risk, per year; the ± is its standard error. Every AI strategy had a smaller worst drop than holding (−31% to −36% vs −40%), because it spent much of the period in cash.

What happened

  • January: all strategies stayed near $1,000. Next 3 days reached about $1,120 in mid-January.
  • The crash (30 Jan – 6 Feb): BNB fell from about $845 to $651. Next 1 day bought at $845.70 on 30 Jan and sold at $651.20 on 6 Feb, a $232 loss on one trade. Next 3 days lost about $337 over its two trades around the crash.
  • February to May: the AI strategies spent much of the time in cash while BNB chopped between about $570 and $680, so their balances held roughly flat.
  • Late May: BNB rose to $711. Next 1 day caught part of it with a +9% final trade, and Next 3 days gained +4.5% on its last trade.
  • Next 4 hours made 82 trades. Many won small amounts, but a few large losses and $129 of fees left it at $694.

How the test worked

  • No look-ahead: at the start of each month the models were retrained only on candles whose outcome was already known, then traded that month.
  • Spot trading: the AI holds either BNB or cash, never shorts, and can buy or sell at any candle close (every 4 hours, or daily for Next 3 days).
  • Fees: 0.1% on every buy and sell, Binance's standard spot fee. Anything still held at the end is sold at the last close, paying the fee.
  • Rules fixed before running, the same as in the earlier backtests, not tuned to this period:

Stop-loss test

A stop-loss would have rescued this period, but it does not help reliably, so it was not added to the AI's advice. Stops were fixed in advance: sell if the price falls 5% or 10% below the buy price (fixed), or 10% below its highest close since buying (trailing). After a stop, the AI only buys again once its buy signal has switched off and back on.

How much to trust it

This test cannot show whether the AI works: five months of one coin leaves every Sharpe ratio uncertain by about ±1.6. The longer walk-forward test (Aug 2021 – Oct 2026, all four coins, 0.1% fees, Next 1 day rules) is more telling:

  • The edge is real but small, and most convincing when the AI follows all four coins together.
  • It mostly comes from avoiding crashes, not from higher returns: the AI sits in cash about half the time, so its worst drops are smaller than holding's.
  • The results are a little optimistic. About eight feature sets and strategy variants were compared on data that includes this period. Corrected for that (deflated Sharpe), the chance of a real edge on BTC falls from 87% to 38%.

Takeaways

  • Use Next 1 day or Next 3 days for decisions, not Next 4 hours. Trading every 4-hour signal paid 13% of the account in fees.
  • Spread across all four coins. The combined result is far more reliable than any single coin.
  • Cut fees by paying them in BNB (0.1% → 0.075%). At this size of edge, fees decide the outcome.
  • Don't expect the AI to dodge sudden crashes. It did not see February's 23% fall coming.
