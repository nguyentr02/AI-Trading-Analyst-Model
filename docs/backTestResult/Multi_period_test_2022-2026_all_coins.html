What Smart's trades taught

All of Smart's trades were analysed by the rule that made them, using 2022–2024 to draw lessons and 2025–2026 to check them.

  • The 7% stop-loss destroyed value. Crypto routinely moves 7% within a normal day, so the stop sold near short-term bottoms: the price was higher a day later 60% of the time.
  • Taking half profit at +10% always worked, in both phases.
  • The buy-lower limit orders almost never filled (2% of buys), so they neither helped nor hurt.
  • Smart changes position too often: 35% of its sells were followed by a re-buy within a day (usually at a lower price, but each round trip pays fees).

How much to trust it

  • Each single 6-month run is noisy (Sharpe uncertain by about ±1.4). The patterns above come from 36 runs per strategy, which is far more reliable than any one run.
  • The 2025–2026 check covers only three half-years (12 runs), mostly falling or sideways, so it confirms the stop lesson but cannot test it in a strong rally.
  • Eleven strategy variants have now been tried on this data; the dashboard's deflated Sharpe accounts for that.

Takeaways

  • Use the AI for protection. Its value is in falling and sideways markets; in a strong rally, holding usually wins.
  • Next 3 days is the best all-round signal: highest average, most runs in profit, fewest trades and fees.
  • Smart is the most defensive choice: the smallest crashes, now without the stop-loss that was costing it.
  • Never trade the Next 4 hours signal on its own: it lost in 72% of runs.
  • Next idea to test, the same way: stay invested when the long-term trend is clearly up, so the AI stops stepping out of rallies. It would be chosen on 2022–2024 and checked on 2025–2026.
